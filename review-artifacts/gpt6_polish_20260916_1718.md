## 1_introduction paragraph 1 — ACCEPTED

**before**

Linear probes are the standard tool for asking what a vision-language model (VLM) represents: fit a classifier to a chosen layer's activations and read out a concept \citep{alain2017understanding,belinkov2022probing}. In medical imaging, probes often succeed---clinical findings such as pleural effusion or cardiomegaly are linearly decodable from the visual representation of general-purpose and medical VLMs \citep{zhu2026lost,pedersen2026probes}. Activation steering promises the converse operation: write the probe direction back into the representation and move the model's behaviour \citep{turner2023steering,panickssery2024caa}. Together, these operations would make a probe direction a handle on the concept: read it to detect, write it to control. This paper asks whether that handle exists.

The projected features are $x=R^{\top}\hvec$, where $R\in\R^{D\times512}$ is the fixed Gaussian projection. Two transformations carry a vector of that projected, train-scaled space back to the consumed space. A classifier coefficient $\boldsymbol\beta$ lifts as $\hat\wvec\propto R\,\mathrm{diag}(1/\boldsymbol s)\boldsymbol\beta$, so that the written direction scores the concept the way the probe does (Equation~\ref{eq:lift}). A displacement $\boldsymbol u$ of that space lifts through its minimum-norm preimage as $\hat\wvec\propto R(R^{\top}R)^{-1}\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol s)\boldsymbol u$, which reproduces the displacement exactly: $R^{\top}\hat\wvec\propto\mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol s)\boldsymbol u$. The two differ because $R^{\top}R$ is not a multiple of the identity. Measured on every ALTDIRD block, the spectrum of $R^{\top}R/D$ is full rank with eigenvalues $\cfAltdirdEigMin$--$\cfAltdirdEigMax$ and condition number \cfAltdirdCondMin{}--\cfAltdirdCondMax{}.
