\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Semantic competition separates readability from clinical attribution: Qwen Effusion's reproducible write effect lacks matched ownership, and Mass's clinical advantage depends on wording and response instructions while larger random effects defeat specificity. Paired-input and exploratory validation studies motivate measuring behavioral opportunity before interpreting closure or testing writes. Clinical attribution requires direction--question--prompt comparisons and mechanism evidence; steering efficacy and diagnostic benefit remain separate questions.
