\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A probe normal can read a clinical concept, write a reproducible behavioural change, and still fail to identify that change with its label. At the consumed final visual block, all three registered cells are robust readers. Qwen Effusion further beats matched random and sham controls in two cohorts, yet semantic competition shows that the maximum over five fixed clinical alternatives is reliably stronger; Nodule realizes the point-estimate maximum. The result separates intervention efficacy from semantic attribution. For medical VLM interpretation, clinically meaningful alternatives tested at the same representation, dose, rows, and endpoint are what turn probe-vector steering from a named perturbation into a direction-specific causal claim.
