\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Successful steering leaves label-specific attribution open to semantic competition. Within the fixed NIH ChestX-ray14 direction bundle and Qwen visual locus, Effusion replicates beyond random and sham controls yet loses to a clinical competitor. Mass gains clinical advantage under both \emph{show} A/B mappings and loses it under \emph{is}, while larger random effects defeat specificity. These findings separate intervention efficacy from relative clinical advantage and expose its wording dependence. The measured advantage establishes neither a mechanism of concept use nor diagnostic benefit.
