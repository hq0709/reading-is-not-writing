\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A decoded clinical concept becomes a causal explanation only when its direction wins the right comparison. At the consumed final visual block, all three registered cells establish robust linear availability. Matched random and sham controls distinguish Qwen Effusion from the two LLaVA cells, and an independent 400-patient cohort reproduces Qwen's non-generic effect. Clinical competition supplies the decisive third rung: a fixed Nodule direction remains reliably stronger. The resulting evidence ladder separates what can be read, what can perturb behaviour, and which clinical direction owns that perturbation. For medical VLM interpretation, semantic alternatives tested at the same representation, dose, rows, and endpoint turn probe-vector steering into a direction-specific causal test.
