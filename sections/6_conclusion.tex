\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Qwen Effusion's reproducible write effect lacks matched ownership; Mass's clinical advantage depends on wording and response instructions while larger random effects defeat specificity. Following pilot screening and text calibration, independent image/reader evidence leaves behavioral opportunity unestablished and reader selectivity unreplicated. Clinical attribution requires direction--question--prompt comparisons and mechanism evidence; steering efficacy and diagnostic benefit remain separate questions.
