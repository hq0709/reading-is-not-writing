\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{A three-rung causal evidence ladder.}
The experiments organize linear availability, generic intervention selectivity, and clinical direction specificity into successive evidentiary levels. A positive probe interval establishes that a restricted readout reliably recovers a label under the patient split and control task. A random/sham pass establishes an effect beyond matched non-semantic vectors. Clinical competition then identifies whether that effect preferentially belongs to the named finding. Qwen traverses the first two rungs in two cohorts; the third reveals the reliably stronger Nodule response. The ladder therefore extracts a sharper causal conclusion from an otherwise convincing steering result.

\paragraph{Semantic competition is the missing control.}
Matched clinical directions are load-bearing when a probe normal carries a semantic name. Random vectors test the ambient geometry, and a coordinate permutation tests one construction artifact, but neither represents a plausible competing clinical direction learned with the same readout and data. In the Qwen cell, random/sham controls establish a robust causal perturbation; the clinical family determines what that perturbation can be called. The practical rule is to compare semantic directions at the same locus, dose, rows, and endpoint, with multiplicity handled over that fixed family.

\paragraph{Scope of the evidence.}
The registered evidence covers NIH ChestX-ray14, two 7B architectures, the consumed final visual block, Effusion in both models, Edema in LLaVA, fixed yes/no prompts, a linear readout, and relative-token probe-normal interventions. It establishes a three-cell dissociation at that locus. The paired decoding intervals span zero, so they leave architecture and concept ordering unresolved. Intermediate loci, nonlinear directions, and other medical image modalities define separate tests rather than extensions of the present estimate.

\paragraph{Data and endpoint limitations.}
ChestX-ray14 labels are mined from reports, with 179 positive Edema test images versus 700 positive Effusion images. The patient split prevents identity leakage but does not remove label noise or acquisition correlations. Mean pooling also does not localize a finding spatially. The behavioural endpoint is the probability assigned to a yes answer under fixed prompts, so the evidence concerns this answer pathway rather than diagnostic accuracy or report quality.

\paragraph{Specificity design.}
The independent supplement tests one discovery-locked Qwen dose against five prospectively fixed clinical normals. Recomputing their maximum inside each bootstrap draw controls the familywise endpoint for that set. A broader clinical vocabulary could contain still stronger or more closely related directions, while alternative doses would constitute new endpoints. The result fails the registered criterion with its complete interval below zero.

\paragraph{Reproducibility and ethics.}
Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} records prompts, row-selection hashes, seeds, doses, model revisions, run identifiers, and GPU wall time. Immutable snapshots and terminal receipts bind each result to its environment and inputs. This work evaluates interpretability evidence, not clinical diagnostic performance. Its safety implication is methodological: a decodable feature should be treated as clinically operative only after intervention controls establish direction specificity.
