\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Clinical competition makes attribution conditional on direction, question, and prompt. Mass exceeds all five clinical competitors under both \emph{show} A/B prompts with positive simultaneous lower bounds, while larger random effects defeat both specificity criteria. Both \emph{is} coded margins are negative. Explicit \emph{show} yes/no has a similar observed margin; paired A/B contrasts span zero. Under \emph{is}, clinical margins improve from the original anchor to explicit yes/no to A/B instructions.

The fresh Consolidation cohort leaves positive paired opportunity unresolved in both models. Measuring natural paired behavior supplies context for component closure; the earlier 50-pair result retains its cohort-specific scope.

The LLaVA validation screen measures frozen-reader availability alongside clean discrimination across all six questions in a shared cohort (Section~\ref{sec:generic-results}). Effusion and Cardiomegaly pass reader screening, but no question meets the clean-margin criterion or qualifies for writing. Effusion has the stronger reader lower bound; Cardiomegaly's is near zero. The reserved write cohort remains untouched, leaving causal write efficacy unevaluated.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Validation selection is exploratory, with intervals unadjusted for selection or multiplicity (Appendix~\ref{app:validation-readout}). The mechanism behind the measurement-stage separation remains unresolved, as does the causal channel underlying concentrated responses and mapping-odd dominance shared by random and sham directions (Appendices~\ref{app:response-diagnostics} and~\ref{app:encoding}).

Metadata-matched longitudinal pairs retain unrecorded differences; positive opportunity and the concordance difference remain unresolved. The Qwen-informed comparison covers native configurations, including training and preprocessing, on shared patients. Concordance gives ties half credit under the finite-precision evaluation configured for BF16 (Appendix~\ref{app:paired-opportunity}).

Discovery reused 200 patients and 20 random controls; crossover energy covers four calibration-eligible questions. Mass confirmation has 12 positives among 200 independent patients: all AUROC-change intervals span zero and Brier error increases in every cell. Paired prompt contrasts are descriptive; intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalence. Scope covers ChestX-ray14, two 7B models, one visual locus, and linear readouts; Mass confirmation fixes model, finding, and dose. Report-derived labels, acquisition correlations, and mean pooling limit clinical interpretation. Bootstrap uncertainty conditions on one direction bundle; small Consolidation displacement limits closure resolution (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}).
