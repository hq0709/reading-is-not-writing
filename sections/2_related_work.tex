\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Probing and its controls.}
Linear classifiers have long been used to track information across network depth \citep{alain2017understanding}. A probe score reflects both the representation and the learner fitted on top of it, which motivates matched-capacity probes and control tasks \citep{hewitt2019designing}, and the probing literature distinguishes information a readout can recover from information the model uses \citep{belinkov2022probing}: on synthetic tasks, high probe accuracy persists for properties the model provably does not need \citep{ravichander2021probing}. Causal erasure pursues the complementary test---remove linearly identified information and measure the behavioural consequence \citep{ravfogel2020null,elazar2021amnesic}. Our readability grade inherits the control-task idea: selectivity is measured against random-label probes of equal capacity fitted within recurring image types.

\paragraph{Steering with probe normals.}
Activation engineering changes intermediate representations at inference time and measures how outputs respond \citep{turner2023steering,panickssery2024caa}; a probe normal is an appealing intervention because it supplies a label and a signed vector from the same representation. Separability-trained concept vectors can, however, absorb distractor signals and diverge from the concept they are meant to model \citep{pahde2025navigating}. Random-direction and sham controls test generic sensitivity and coordinate dependence, but not whether an equally constructed direction with a different label would move the same answer more. Ownership adds that comparison, evaluates it in the forward path at the consumed locus, and uses each token's norm as the dose scale so that writes are comparable across layers and architectures.

\paragraph{Medical VLM interpretation.}
Depth-wise probing of medical multimodal models has mapped gains and losses of classification information across vision towers, connectors, and language layers \citep{zhu2026lost}, and layer-wise probes of MedCLIP expose acquisition and device shortcuts in chest radiographs \citep{pedersen2026probes}. These studies motivate tight nuisance controls but do not intervene on the decoded clinical direction at the same locus, and no prior work grades concept writability across model families. Our evaluation closes that gap with matched reading, non-semantic write controls, semantic competition, and a natural-image control, applied at scale.
