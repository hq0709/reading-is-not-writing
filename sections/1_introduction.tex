\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

A linear probe reads a concept; steering assigns it a writing role. Qwen's Effusion normal reproducibly beats random and sham controls under original yes/no prompts, yet Nodule moves the Effusion answer more. Mass's clinical advantage under \emph{show} A/B prompts reverses under \emph{is}. Attribution depends on direction, question, and prompt.

Probes expose information available to restricted readouts \citep{alain2017understanding,hewitt2019designing}, without establishing model use \citep{ravichander2021probing}. Semantic competition tests whether a direction is privileged among equally constructed clinical alternatives.

For fixed yes/no prompts, $W_{q,d}$ is the paired mean change in question $q$'s yes probability under direction $d$. Ownership $O_q=W_{q,q}-\max_{d\neq q}W_{q,d}$ compares the named direction with its strongest clinical competitor (Figure~\ref{fig:framework}). Contenders share construction, locus, dose, patients, and endpoint. Finding-present logit margins align answer mappings.

Six gates establish the reader--writer boundary on NIH ChestX-ray14: three LLaVA/Qwen cells have positive controlled selectivity, but neither LLaVA cell clears random/sham controls. Qwen's six-direction by six-question matrix yields zero owned concepts and no shared alias beyond 119 random directions. A 50-patient Consolidation test finds no reliable input-displacement closure.

Independent Mass confirmation establishes positive simultaneous clinical margins under both \emph{show} A/B prompts, while larger effects among 119 random directions defeat both registered specificity criteria. Both \emph{is} coded margins are negative; explicit \emph{show} yes/no has a similar observed margin (Section~\ref{sec:encoding-results}).

Behavioral opportunity completes this chain. On the same 100 fresh Consolidation patient pairs, both models leave positive natural paired opportunity unresolved (Section~\ref{sec:paired-opportunity}). An exploratory LLaVA validation screen separates frozen-reader availability from clean answer discrimination at the measurement stage (Section~\ref{sec:generic-results}). Together, these studies motivate measuring behavioral opportunity alongside readability and clinical competition before interpreting component closure or pursuing causal writes.
