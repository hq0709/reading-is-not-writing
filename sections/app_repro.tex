\subsection{The consumed-token mask}
\label{app:repro-mask}

The write of Equation~\ref{eq:intervention} is applied to the token positions the consumer reads at that locus and to no other entry of the hooked tensor. The adapter derives those positions for each batch from the processor's own outputs. Two tensor shapes occur. The Qwen family hands the merger a flat $(N,D)$ tensor in which every image occupies one contiguous slice, and the adapter computes the slice bounds from \texttt{image\_grid\_thw} (the connector slice is a quarter of the length, one merged token per $2\times2$ patch group). Every other family hands the consumer a batched $(B,T,D)$ tensor, and the adapter supplies one boolean mask per batch element:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item Gemma~3 and MedGemma: all 4{,}096 patch rows; the tower produces no CLS row. The mask length is checked against the count of \texttt{<image\_soft\_token>} placeholders in the language-model input.
\item InternVL3.5: index 0 is the CLS row, which the consumer drops, so it is excluded; the remaining rows of the single $448\times448$ tile are written. The mask length is checked against the count of \texttt{<IMG\_CONTEXT>} placeholders.
\item LLaVA-1.5 and LLaVA-Med: index 0 is the CLS row, which the checkpoint's \texttt{vision\_}\allowbreak\texttt{feature\_}\allowbreak\texttt{select\_}\allowbreak\texttt{strategy} drops under its \texttt{default} setting, so it is excluded; rows 1 to 576 are written.
\item Llama~3.2 Vision: the 1{,}601 rows (CLS and 1{,}600 patches) of the one real $560\times560$ tile. The seven zero-padding rows of each tile and the three padding tiles are excluded, and the mask is checked against \texttt{aspect\_ratio\_mask} and the last position of \texttt{cross\_attention\_mask}.
\end{itemize}

The hook raises rather than falls back: it raises when a flat layout does not cover the hooked tensor exactly, when a mask length differs from the token axis, when a batch element has no consumed token, and when the hooked module fires more than once in one forward.

Preflight check (C) verifies the mask. It captures the locus tensor on a clean pass and on a pass steered at $\alpha=+0.25$ along the first concept direction, and reports the largest absolute difference outside the mask, the largest absolute change in the consumer's output, and the largest absolute change in the answer logits. Across all \cfBlocks{} blocks the change outside the consumed tokens is $0$ at both loci, while the consumer's output and the answer logits both change. The same files record a bitwise-identical repeated clean pass (check A) and a bitwise-identical $\alpha=0$ pass (check B) for every block.

\subsection{Projection, lift, scaler, and probe}
\label{app:repro-probe}

The projection $R\in\R^{D\times512}$ is one draw of \texttt{PCG64(0).standard\_normal((D,512))} divided by $\sqrt{512}$ and stored in float32, so the entries are independent $\mathcal{N}(0,1/512)$ variates. The seed is $0$ for every checkpoint, dataset, and locus; $D$ is the family's hidden dimension. The columns of $R$ are neither normalised nor orthogonalised, and the lifted direction of Equation~\ref{eq:lift} is normalised once, in model space, to unit $\ell_2$ norm. The gram matrix $R^{\top}R/D$ is full rank at 512 in every one of the \cfAltdirdBlocks{} blocks that carry the displacement module, with condition number \cfAltdirdCondMin{}--\cfAltdirdCondMax{}; Table~\ref{tab:cf-altdird} reports its eigenvalue range.

Projected features are $R^{\top}\hvec_i$ for the token-mean read vector $\hvec_i$. The featurewise scale $\boldsymbol s$ and mean come from a \texttt{StandardScaler} fitted on the training rows alone, and every row is standardised as $(R^{\top}\hvec-\boldsymbol\mu)\oslash\max(\boldsymbol s,10^{-8})$. Each concept probe is \texttt{LogisticRegression} with $C=1$, \texttt{solver="lbfgs"}, \texttt{max\_iter=2000}, \texttt{class\_weight=None}, and \texttt{random\_state=0}, fitted on the training rows whose label for that concept is known. The positive-class coefficient fixes the clinical pole of the direction. The two refit seeds resample the training units with replacement, keep every row of a sampled unit at its multiplicity, and refit the scaler and the six probes against the same projection; the random family, the shams, and the control probes stay at seed 0.

\subsection{Control constructions}
\label{app:repro-controls}

\paragraph{Random directions.} One \texttt{PCG64(0)} draw of a $119\times D$ standard-normal matrix, each row normalised to unit $\ell_2$ norm. The same 119 directions serve every question of the block.

\paragraph{Coordinate-permutation sham.} The same generator, immediately after the random draw, produces one permutation of $\{0,\dots,D{-}1\}$ per concept in protocol concept order. Concept $c$'s sham is $\hat\wvec_c$ with its coordinates rearranged by concept $c$'s own permutation, so the sham carries the same coordinate multiset and the same unit norm as the direction it shams. Each question is written with its own sham.

\paragraph{Matched random-label probes.} Each row carries a type identifier: view $\times$ sex $\times$ age decade on the chest sets, and aspect-ratio $\times$ area buckets on COCO. Control seed $k\in\{0,\dots,19\}$ walks the sorted type names and draws one label from \texttt{PCG64($k$).integers(0,2)} for each; a draw that assigns one label to every type has its first entry flipped. Every row of a type receives that type's label. Each control probe is then fitted on the same training rows, the same known-label subset, and the same hyperparameters as the concept probe, which gives it the concept probe's capacity and nuisance structure. A block fits control probes when its training rows span at least eight types. Selectivity is Equation~\ref{eq:selectivity}. Its bootstrap over calibration units keeps a draw when the real AUROC is finite and at least ten of the twenty control AUROCs are finite, and a cell is graded for readability when at least 1{,}900 of the 2{,}000 draws are kept.

\subsection{Protocol record and amendments}

All \cfBlocks{} blocks carry the protocol identifier \texttt{cf-transfer-v1}. The protocol file registers its own code reference commit \texttt{763072d3}.
